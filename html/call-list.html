How sites are ranked (ASSUMPTION)

The ranking is the team's judgment, not from a law or program. Only parcels the screen marked "Possible" are listed (SB 1123: vacant single-family lots; SB 4: church or college land use, a proxy for ownership). Flood-zone parcels and SCAG "parent" outlines are left out. AB 2011 and SB 684 rank vacant land and parking first, then retail and office, then other non-housing uses, then existing housing (tenants may live there); bigger lots first within each group. SB 9, SB 1123 and SB 4 rank by lot size. Rules are in lanes/1-find-it/scripts/rank_sites.py.

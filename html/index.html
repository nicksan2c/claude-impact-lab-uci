Ten Sites to Call

Affordable-housing site leads for one Orange County city, from public data, with the reasons and the gaps shown.

Prototype. Built in one day at the Claude Impact Lab. Not legal, financial or lending advice. Every site is a lead to check, not a confirmed opportunity. Confirm zoning, ownership and hazards with the city and a professional before acting.
Blue: housing-element site. Orange: surplus-land notice. Number = rank.
